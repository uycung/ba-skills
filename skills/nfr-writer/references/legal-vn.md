# legal-vn: legal reference for NFRs (Vietnam, with an EU section)

Last verified: 24/10/2026. Main sources: full texts downloaded from the Official Gazette (congbao.chinhphu.vn) and EUR-Lex, read by the agent on 24/10/2026. For the Vietnam part there is only **one original source** (thuvienphapluat.vn and vbpl.moj.gov.vn were not accessible), so there is no independent cross-check yet. This is not legal advice: legal counsel must confirm before it goes into a contract or spec.
Updated 24/10/2026 (third round: added Decision 33/2026/QĐ-TTg and the Decree 330/2026 penalty bands from secondary sources; second round: added Decree 330/2026 and Decree 142/2026 from a partial excerpt on thuvienphapluat.vn (page cut off midway, ⚠️), and recorded that the compiled legal file supplied by the user is **not used as a source** (see the end of the file).)
Convention: ✅ the original source was read in full; ⚠️ only a secondary source, a partial excerpt, or not yet found.
Terms: Law = Luật; Decree = Nghị định (NĐ); Circular = Thông tư (TT); Decision = Quyết định (QĐ). "Art." = Điều (article); "(n)" after an article number = clause (khoản) n. Amounts are in Vietnamese dong (VND).

The excerpt of Art. 8(4) on thuvienphapluat refers to Decree 118/2021 (amended by Decrees 68/2025 and 190/2025) about conditions for electronic procedures, which is only a reference, not a repeal.
Attach to characteristics: Security (Confidentiality, Accountability), Interaction capability (consent, transparency), Reliability/Recoverability (breach response).

|---|---|---|

| Decree 53/2022 | Appears only in Art. 31 of Decree 333: files submitted under Decree 53/2022 are still handled under that decree. **It could not be confirmed that Decree 53/2022 has expired** (a draft said it would expire; the issued text does not) | ⚠️ |

Attach to characteristics: Security (all), Reliability (system level, incident response).

## How to use in the skill
1. Ask whether the product processes personal data, payment cards, AI, serves the EU market, or is an important system.
2. For each "yes", add an obligation NFR with the instrument name, article number and the line "needs legal confirmation".

## Legal compilation file supplied by the user (Vietnam_Legal_Compliance_NFR_Architecture_Guide): not used as a source
Cross-checked against the content above (read from the original texts or excerpts). The file's content was not put into the skill for the following reasons:
- It treats **Decree 13/2023 as "in force"**. The content above (read from the Official Gazette) shows Decree 356/2025 replaces Decree 13/2023 from 01/01/2026. This is a foundational error, affecting breach notification deadlines, data subject rights, and the impact assessment file.
- Its per-act fine bands (20–40, 30–50, 40–60 million VND) **do not match** press reports and could not be checked against the full text; only the 30–50 million band for collection without consent coincides with press reports. Do not use these figures to write NFRs.
- It treats **Decree 53/2022 and Decree 85/2016 as "in force"**, and writes data storage per Art. 26 of Decree 53/2022. The validity of these two instruments is unconfirmed (⚠️ above); Decree 333/2026 Art. 19–20 is the instrument actually read on data storage.
- It says Circular 12/2022/TT-BTTTT **replaces** Circular 03/2017 and lists specific technical requirements under that circular; whether it replaces or amends is unconfirmed, and the technical requirements have no verifiable origin.
- The "cross-check sources" table in the file is the compiler's assertion, not the result of a check that was actually carried out.
Use this file only as a list of suggested questions for legal counsel, not as a source for figures, validity or provisions.

## Two later legal files and the agent's log (Decree 330/2026, Decree 142/2026 + Decision 33/2026): not used as sources
Two ".md" files that call themselves "full-text structure" and the agent's work log are **compilations**, not original texts. Comparison with public sources shows:
- The article numbers **do not match** the excerpts that carry the real text: the file says Art. 3 is the form of penalty and the maximum level, and Art. 29–48 are personal data protection (Decree 330); the excerpt on thuvienphapluat has Art. 4 as the form of penalty, Art. 7 as the maximum level, and Section 3 before Art. 25. For Decree 142, the file says Art. 6 is the high-risk list, 46 articles, 3 appendices; the excerpt has Art. 7 as the list, Art. 8 as the criteria, Art. 9 medium risk, and appendix forms AI01 to AI09. Do not use article numbers from these two files.
- **Decision 33/2026 list: the file lists 8 groups** (including recruitment and HR, personal credit scoring, high-frequency trading, ICU, biometric identification in public places) with a "control requirements" column (ISO 13485, IEC 62304, IEC 61508, ISO 26262, 36-month log retention...) that **does not match** the excerpt of the list (6 groups, 16 items, with no recruitment/HR). No source was found for those control requirements. Treat as unreliable.
- The penalty bands in the Decree 330 file differ from the two secondary sources on many acts (impact assessment file 30–50 vs 20–30; DPO 20–40 vs 20–30; sensitive data 50–80; cross-border 40–60 and 80–100). Only the consent band 30–50 coincides. The file also cites Decree 13/2023 and its "Form No. 04" as a basis, while Decree 356/2025 has replaced Decree 13/2023.
- The agent log states "Decree 53/2022 has expired" and "Decree 85/2016, Circular 12/2022 remain in force": no verifiable source (see the Still open section).
- Two items **usable with conditions**: the issue/effective dates (Decree 330 on 19/08/2026, Decision 33 effective 15/08/2026) because they match independent sources.
