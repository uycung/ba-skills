# PRD - Clinic booking
Approved.

## Stakeholders
Patients, the clinic manager, front-desk staff and the IT team. The sponsor is the clinic owner.

## Business case
The clinic expects to save 3 hours of phone work per day.

## Requirements
- US-02 A patient may hold at most one appointment per doctor per day.
- AC-01.2 When two patients confirm the same slot, only the first confirmation succeeds.
- NFR-PERF-02 Search responds within 3 seconds.
