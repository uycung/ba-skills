## NFRs for online booking

ISO 25010 gives a classification, not numeric thresholds, so I did not pick numbers. Each number is for the PO to fix.

| Code | Characteristic | Measure | Threshold | Evidence type | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NFR-PERF-01 | Performance efficiency | Response time of the booking confirmation | [PO to fix] | (c) common practice, needs the PO | Tech lead |
| NFR-SEC-01 | Security | Encryption of patient data | [PO to fix] | (c) common practice, needs the PO | Security |
| NFR-REL-01 | Reliability | Availability per month | [PO to fix] | (c) common practice, needs the PO | Operations |
