# Business case: online booking (3-year view)

I am not a financial or legal adviser. This is analysis to support the decision, not investment advice.

## Options
| Option | Description |
|---|---|
| Do nothing | Keep phone booking: no build cost, the reception workload stays. |
| Online booking | Build once, run it for 3 years. |

## Figures
- Build cost: 12,000 USD one-off [Stated]
- Running cost: 1,500 USD a year [Stated]
- Saving: 400 hours of reception time a year [Stated]
- One hour of reception time: 8 USD [Stated]
- Period: 3 years [Stated]
